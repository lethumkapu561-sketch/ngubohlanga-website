Company Overview

NGUBOHLANGA MULTI-SERVICES (PTY) LTD is a South African construction company committed to delivering quality, reliable and sustainable construction solutions.


The company operates with a strong focus on infrastructure development and building construction, aiming to contribute meaningfully to community development, job creation and economic growth.


We combine technical skills, practical experience and professional workmanship to deliver projects that meet client expectations, regulatory standards and industry best practices.
